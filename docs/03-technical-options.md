# Technical Options

## Option A: Modify Official Instagram on Non-Jailbroken iOS

- **Status:** Not feasible.
- **Reason:** iOS platform protections block cross-app UI/control changes.

## Option B: Modify Official Instagram on Jailbroken iOS

- **Status:** Feasible in principle.
- **Approach:** Tweak/injection to remove tabs, filter surfaces, and block reel chaining.
- **Tradeoffs:** Smaller audience, operational complexity, higher maintenance risk.

## Option C: Separate Reels-Free Client Experience on iOS

- **Status:** Feasible in principle.
- **Approach:** Build a dedicated app experience that enforces no-reels UX.
- **Tradeoffs:** Product behavior differs from modifying the official Instagram app.

## Option D: Platform Shift for Official-App Modification

- **Status:** Potentially feasible depending on platform tooling.
- **Approach:** Build where official app behavior can be altered with accepted distribution method.
