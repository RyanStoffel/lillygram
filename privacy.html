Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Lillygram ("the app") is an independent, personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc. "Instagram" is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc.

What the app is

The app is an in-app browser (a standard iOS WKWebView) pointed at instagram.com, with the user's own real Instagram login. It layers on top of Instagram's own web client to hide specific features (the algorithmic feed, Reels discovery, content search) and to show a home feed limited to accounts on the user's own official Instagram Favorites list. It does not create a separate account, store your Instagram password, or provide any Instagram functionality of its own — everything you see is Instagram's real website, running with your real session.

What data the app accesses

The app runs entirely on your device. It has no backend server of its own, and no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK is included.

  • Your Instagram session. Login cookies are managed by iOS's standard WKWebView storage, the same mechanism Safari uses — the app does not read, copy, or transmit your password or session tokens anywhere itself.
  • Your Favorites picks. The list of accounts you select in the app's picker is stored locally on your device (UserDefaults). It is used only to keep Instagram's own, official Favorites list in sync with your picks, via the same kind of request Instagram's own web client makes when you use its "Add/Remove Favorites" feature. This list is not sent anywhere except to Instagram's own servers, using your own logged-in session.
  • Feed data. To build a favorites-only home feed, the app reads your own home/favorites feed data from Instagram's website using your existing session, the same data your device would receive browsing instagram.com normally.

Bug reports

The in-app "Report a Bug" button opens your device's own Mail app with a pre-filled draft you can review, edit, or discard before choosing to send it yourself. Nothing is collected or transmitted automatically.

Third parties

The app does not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party. Your use of Instagram itself remains subject to Instagram's own privacy policy, since the app is simply displaying and interacting with Instagram's real website on your behalf.

Children

The app is not directed at children and requires a valid Instagram account, which per Instagram's own terms requires being at least 13 years old.
